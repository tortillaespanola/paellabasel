Vegetarian Spanish Menu

A Spanish-inspired vegetarian menu based on seasonal vegetables, extra virgin olive oil and traditional Spanish cooking for 20 guests.

Dietary Requirement Note: All dishes in this proposal are vegetarian and, according to the specific requirements provided, contain no eggs, cheese, mushrooms, aubergine or citrus fruits.
Main Course

🥘 Vegetarian Paella (20 guests)

CHF 580.–

A traditional Spanish paella prepared with seasonal vegetables, Spanish rice, a traditional sofrito, extra virgin olive oil and homemade-style vegetable stock.
Prepared using two paella pans (one 60 cm and one 40 cm) to maintain a proper rice-to-pan ratio and achieve an even, high-quality cooking result. Equivalent to CHF 29.– per person.

Main Ingredients:
  • Spanish rice
  • Artichokes
  • Cauliflower
  • Green beans
  • Red peppers
  • Tomato
  • Garlic
  • Sweet paprika
  • Saffron
  • Vegetable stock
  • Extra virgin olive oil
Side Dishes & Accompaniments

🫑 Escalivada

CHF 75.–

Roasted red and yellow peppers and onion, dressed with extra virgin olive oil, garlic and sea salt.
Approximate quantity: 1.5–1.7 kg prepared.

🫘 White Beans in Escabeche

CHF 75.–

White beans marinated in a mild Spanish-style escabeche with onion, carrot, garlic and bay leaf, prepared with olive oil and wine vinegar. A fresh and flavourful dish that can be prepared in advance and served cold or at room temperature.
Approximate quantity: 1.4–1.5 kg prepared.

🥔 Spanish Vegetable Russian Salad

CHF 80.–

A vegetarian version of the classic Spanish ensaladilla rusa, prepared without egg, tuna or peas.
Approximate quantity: 1.5–1.7 kg.

Ingredients:
  • Potato
  • Carrot
  • Green beans
  • Capers
  • Pickles
  • Olives
  • Vegan mayonnaise

🍞 Bread

CHF 30.–

Selection of bread to accompany the side dishes.
Approximate quantity: 1.5 kg.
